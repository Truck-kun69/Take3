# Shardfall: Rift Survivors — Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026.

Shardfall: Rift Survivors does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data.

- **No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.** The game contains no advertising or analytics code and makes no
  network connections.
- **One permission: vibration**, for haptic feedback. You can turn it off in Options › Game › Haptics.
- **Your progress stays on your phone.** Gold, gear, unlocks and settings are saved in the app's own storage on
  your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
- **Copy save and Copy report act only when you tap them.** Copy save puts your progress on your phone's
  clipboard as text. Copy report puts your phone model, graphics settings, last benchmark and the game's recent
  log on the clipboard. Neither sends anything anywhere; where you paste the text is your choice.
- **Children.** The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

Questions: use the developer contact email on the game's Google Play page.
