# Texture credits

All texture sets here are **CC0** (public domain) and were resized to 512 px and repacked
(colour, OpenGL normal, and AO/roughness/metal in one "ORM" image) by `tools/blender/pack_textures.py`.
Cloth, velvet and stone are desaturated so the models can tint them; steel and chain are lifted for
the game's dark scenes. No attribution is required; credit is given anyway.
