# Prop model credits

`crystal`, `pillar`, `tree`, `rock` and `bones` (.glb) were generated for Shardfall: a concept image from FLUX.1 [schnell],
turned into a 3D model by TRELLIS 2 (Microsoft, MIT licence), both run on fal.ai. Then
`tools/blender/import_props.py` reduced, recoloured and tagged them. The concept images are in
`tools/fal/concepts/`. Check fal.ai's terms for commercial use of generated outputs.
